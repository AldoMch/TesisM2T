# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## 1. Introduction

### Purpose
This SRS defines the requirements for the "Zhiyan Tongjie" (智研通界) Academic Achievement Sharing Platform. It specifies the platform's business functions, external interfaces, data requirements, quality attributes, and technical constraints, and provides a basis for architecture design, implementation, and acceptance testing.

### Product scope
The platform provides researchers, scholars, and academic institutions with an integrated environment for discovering, managing, sharing, and discussing academic achievements. It combines literature retrieval with scholarly portals, verified achievement ownership, institution management, and academic communication.

The scope includes:
- Account registration, authentication, scholar certification, and profile management.
- Multidimensional and multilingual literature retrieval, conversational search, filtering, and ranking.
- Personalized literature presentation and cross-disciplinary recommendations.
- Academic achievement classification, verified claiming, and ownership dispute resolution.
- Institution creation, membership approval, and hierarchical organization management.
- Comments, questions, private messages, scholar following, collaborative annotations, and report sharing.
- Academic data collection, normalization, daily incremental synchronization, and analysis.
- Access authorization, delayed disclosure, academic misconduct detection, persistent storage, and backup.

### Intended audience
- Researchers and institutional stakeholders validating platform behavior.
- Product managers and analysts maintaining the requirements.
- Architects and developers designing and implementing the platform.
- Test engineers verifying functionality, performance, security, and recoverability.

### Definitions
- **Academic achievement:** A scholarly output, including a publication or literature record, with author, institution, classification, and visibility information.
- **Certified scholar:** A registered user whose scholar identity application has been approved.
- **Achievement claim:** A request to associate a certified scholar with an existing achievement after identity and authorship verification.
- **Unpublished achievement:** An achievement whose protected content requires institutional authorization for access.
- **Delayed disclosure:** A restriction preventing public release of designated sensitive information for three calendar months after its generation date.

## 2. Overall Description

### Product perspective
The platform is a browser-based academic resource sharing and management system. It obtains scholar and literature data from public academic databases through crawlers and APIs, maintains normalized records, and exposes discovery, management, and communication functions through a web interface.

The system separates user-facing business logic in an application layer, shared academic services in a support layer, and computing, acquisition, and persistence responsibilities in an infrastructure layer. The application layer uses common semantic processing, profiling, authorization, and analysis services to deliver consistent behavior across business functions.

### Product functions summary
- Maintain accounts, certified scholar identities, personal portals, and institutional affiliations.
- Search literature by keywords, disciplines, institutions, and authors using semantic correction, cross-language mapping, and conversational context.
- Filter and rank results, adapt presentation to user profiles, and recommend researchers across disciplines.
- Classify achievements, verify author claims, and resolve ownership disputes through preliminary, expert, and management review.
- Manage institution applications and university, institute, and laboratory relationships.
- Support moderated discussions, verified private messaging, following, annotations, and report sharing.
- Collect and normalize external data, synchronize incremental updates daily, and generate academic analysis outputs.
- Enforce access and disclosure policies, protect data, detect potential misconduct, and maintain recoverable backups.

### User classes

| User class | Responsibilities and access |
|---|---|
| Visitor | Browse and search public academic content and register an account. |
| Registered user | Authenticate, use permitted interaction and personalization functions, and apply for scholar certification. |
| Certified scholar | Maintain a scholarly profile, claim achievements, and apply for institution membership in addition to registered-user functions. |
| Institution administrator | Review membership applications, verify institutional associations, and authorize access to unpublished achievements within the assigned institution. |
| Expert reviewer | Examine assigned escalated academic disputes and record review findings. |
| System administrator | Review scholar certification and institution creation requests, administer classifications and institutional structures, moderate content, and perform final dispute handling. |

### Operating environment
- Users access the platform through Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on Windows, Linux, or macOS.
- The frontend uses Vue 3.3, and the backend uses Python with Django 4.2 LTS.
- Structured persistence uses MySQL 8.0 with cluster support, complemented by storage for unstructured academic content.
- Server-side deployment supports Linux or Windows.

### Assumptions and dependencies
- External academic databases provide crawler or API access to scholar profiles and literature metadata.
- Scholar and institutional verification depend on supporting information and authorized reviewers.
- Semantic search, translation, profiling, and misconduct detection use academic terminology resources and configured processing rules or models.
- Imported metadata, self-edited profile information, and verified ownership or membership relationships remain distinguishable throughout processing.

## 3. External Interface Requirements

### User interfaces

| Interface | Required interactions and displayed information |
|---|---|
| Account and personal portal | Registration and login; certification submission and status; editable profile information; scholar achievements and cooperation networks. |
| Literature discovery | Keyword and natural-language input; conversational follow-up input; discipline, institution, and author criteria; filter and sort controls; result lists, achievement details, and recommendations. |
| Achievement management | Classification browsing; claim submission and status; verification information; ownership objections and dispute decisions. |
| Institution management | Institution creation and membership requests; application decisions; viewing and authorized editing of institution hierarchies. |
| Academic communication | Comments and moderation status; questions and replies; private conversations; follow/unfollow actions; collaborative annotations and shared reports. |
| Administration | Certification and institution review queues; content moderation; dispute handling; access authorization; collection and synchronization status. |

Forms shall identify missing or invalid required input. Restricted operations shall indicate the required authorization. Empty search results, pending reviews, failed requests, and successful operations shall have distinguishable states.

### Software/API interfaces
- External acquisition interfaces shall retrieve scholar profiles and literature metadata through academic database APIs and crawlers, preserving source identifiers for normalization and incremental updates.
- The browser/backend interface shall support account, retrieval, achievement, institution, interaction, and administrative operations and enforce the corresponding authorization rules.
- Semantic processing interfaces shall accept query text, language, and applicable conversational context and provide interpreted terms or retrieval signals for search and recommendation.
- Persistence interfaces shall support structured records and associated unstructured academic content.
- Backup and restore interfaces shall preserve both stored content and its relationships with structured records.

### Communication interfaces
Authenticated browser/backend communication and private-message transport shall use encrypted channels. Private conversations shall be accessible only to authorized participants. External acquisition interfaces shall handle connection failures without reporting incomplete retrieval as a successful synchronization.

### Data exchange formats
Academic data exchange shall preserve source identifiers, original language, bibliographic metadata, and applicable visibility information. Text input, transfer, storage, and display shall preserve Chinese, English, Japanese, and German characters. Translated content shall retain an association with its original record.

## 4. Functional Requirements

### Accounts and scholarly portals

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Account registration | When a visitor submits a username, password, and required personal information, the system shall validate the input and create a registered-user account. Missing required fields or an existing username shall prevent creation and produce a validation message. |
| FR-002 | Authentication and session management | When a user submits valid credentials, the system shall authenticate the user and grant access according to assigned roles. Invalid credentials shall not establish a session. Logout shall invalidate the user's active session. |
| FR-003 | Scholar certification | When a logged-in user submits scholar verification information, the system shall create an application, route it to an administrator, and display its status and decision. Scholar permissions shall be granted only after approval. |
| FR-004 | Profile management | When a scholar edits nickname, email, or affiliation information, the system shall validate and save permitted changes. Editing affiliation text shall not itself approve institutional membership or achievement ownership. |
| FR-005 | Scholar portal viewing | When a user opens a scholar's portal, the system shall display permitted basic information, academic achievements, and cooperation networks, applying the visibility rules of the underlying records. |

### Literature retrieval and recommendations

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-006 | Multidimensional retrieval | When a user supplies keyword, discipline, institution, or author criteria, the system shall search across the supplied criteria and display matching permitted literature. A valid query without matches shall return an explicit empty result. |
| FR-007 | Semantic and multilingual retrieval | For keyword and natural-language queries, the system shall apply semantic error correction and cross-language academic term mapping. It shall support Chinese, English, Japanese, and German literature retrieval and translation-assisted presentation while preserving original records. |
| FR-008 | Multi-turn search | When a user submits a follow-up request, the system shall use the active search conversation's prior turns to interpret the request and refine results. Starting a new conversation shall clear prior conversational constraints. |
| FR-009 | Filtering and ranking | When a user changes filters or sorting, the system shall refresh results using the selected criteria. It shall support relevance, citation count, publication time, and academic credibility ranking. Restricted records shall remain excluded. |
| FR-010 | Personalized presentation and recommendation | The system shall use user profiles to personalize content and presentation, including deductive, inductive, and visual preferences. Users without an established profile shall receive a default presentation and retain ordinary search access. |
| FR-011 | Cross-disciplinary discovery | When repeated same-field searches satisfy the configured diversity rule, the system shall include potentially relevant researchers from other disciplines in recommendations while preserving the user's explicit search criteria. |

### Academic achievements and institutions

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-012 | Academic classification | The system shall allow authorized administrators to maintain multilevel academic field classifications and achievement associations. It shall preserve valid references and reject cyclic parent relationships. Users shall be able to retrieve achievements through the classifications. |
| FR-013 | Achievement claiming | When a certified scholar claims an existing achievement, the system shall verify identity, authorship, and any institutional association used as evidence before binding an approved claim. Failed or disputed claims shall not overwrite verified relationships. An achievement shall support multiple legitimate coauthors. |
| FR-014 | Ownership disputes | When a user or scholar submits an ownership objection, the system shall record supporting information and support preliminary, expert, and management final review, escalating cases that remain unresolved. It shall preserve evidence, stage decisions, and the final outcome. Ownership changes shall be traceable to an authorized decision. |
| FR-015 | Institution applications | When a user requests institution creation or a certified scholar requests membership, the system shall verify eligibility and route the request to the appropriate system or institution administrator. Only approved applications shall create active institutions or membership relationships. Applicants shall be able to view status and decisions. |
| FR-016 | Institution hierarchy management | The system shall allow authorized administrators to maintain the university/institute/laboratory hierarchy and approved memberships. It shall reject cyclic relationships and modifications outside the administrator's institutional scope. |

### Academic interaction and collaboration

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-017 | Moderated comments | When a registered user or scholar submits a comment on an achievement, the system shall store it for moderation and publicly display it only after approval. Pending or rejected comments shall not appear as approved public comments. |
| FR-018 | Academic questions and replies | When a user asks an author a question from a literature detail page, the system shall associate it with the achievement and addressed author and allow that author to reply. Questions and replies shall remain linked as a discussion. |
| FR-019 | Verified private messaging | Before enabling private messaging, the system shall verify that both participants satisfy identity requirements. It shall transmit messages through an encrypted channel and restrict conversation access to participants. Failed verification shall block sending and provide an explanation. |
| FR-020 | Scholar following | When a logged-in user follows or unfollows a scholar, the system shall persist or remove the relationship and prevent duplicate relationships between the same user and scholar. |
| FR-021 | Collaborative annotations and report sharing | The system shall support annotations associated with academic resources and sharing of analysis reports. It shall enforce participant and resource permissions; collaboration and sharing shall not bypass restrictions on underlying content. |

### Academic data acquisition and analysis

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-022 | External data acquisition | Scheduled collection processes shall obtain scholar profiles and literature records from configured public academic databases through crawlers and APIs. The system shall retain source provenance and record collection results and failures. |
| FR-023 | Data normalization and deduplication | The system shall standardize, deduplicate, and structure collected data before incorporating it into the corpus. Reprocessing a source record shall not duplicate a logical achievement or erase verified scholar associations. Invalid records shall be identifiable for correction. |
| FR-024 | Daily incremental synchronization | The system shall synchronize new and updated academic records daily and record each run's outcome. Failed or incomplete runs shall be identifiable, and retries shall not duplicate previously imported records. |
| FR-025 | Academic analysis | Scheduled analysis shall generate research hotspot reports and scholar influence profiles from collected academic data. Outputs shall identify the summarized data period and follow visibility and disclosure rules when displayed or shared. |

### Data protection and academic integrity

| ID | Requirement | Trigger, system behavior, and output |
|---|---|---|
| FR-026 | Unpublished achievement authorization | When a user requests unpublished content, the system shall require authorization from the responsible institution administrator. It shall enforce this rule on direct requests, portal views, search results, and shared resources without disclosing protected content to unauthorized users. |
| FR-027 | Delayed disclosure | The system shall prevent public disclosure of designated sensitive information, including influence rankings, until three calendar months after its generation date. Expiry of the delay shall not override separate resource access restrictions. |
| FR-028 | Academic misconduct detection | The system shall support draft plagiarism checking and blacklist filtering and associate results with the checked items. Flagged results shall be available to authorized reviewers. A failed check shall remain distinguishable from a completed clean result. |

### Data lifecycle and consistency
- Certification and institution applications shall distinguish pending, approved, and rejected states and retain their decisions.
- Claims shall distinguish unverified requests from approved author associations. Rejecting a claim shall not delete an existing approved coauthor relationship.
- Imported metadata, self-edited profile text, and administrator-verified relationships shall remain distinguishable. Imports shall not silently replace approval decisions.
- Access restrictions shall apply consistently to stored content and derived views, including search results, recommendations, reports, annotations, and backups.
- Daily full and hourly incremental backups shall preserve a usable restoration chain throughout the 30-day retention period.
